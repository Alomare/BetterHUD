# Better HUD

A mod for HELLDIVERS (2015) that adds to the game's HUD, for [Obsidian Mod Manager](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianModManager). Each part can be turned on or off in the pause menu, under **Mod Settings → Better HUD**.

- **Ammo counter:** the rounds left in your magazine, over the magazine count.
- **Player health bars:** a red bar under each player's card, in missions. A white chunk fades out where health was lost, as the game's own bars do. A downed player's bar turns dark red as they bleed out, with a yellow bar under it showing how close they are to getting back up.
- **Enemy health bars:** a bar over every damaged enemy, or only damaged heavies. Bugs' bars are orange, cyborgs' red and the Illuminate's purple. White chunks fade out where health was lost, the chunk turns orange while the enemy burns and green while it's poisoned, and the bar pulses blue while it's stunned. Ticks mark every 100 health, bigger enemies get bigger bars, and a bar flashes dark red when its enemy dies. Bars follow the game's UI scale setting.
- **Bars over Helldivers:** the game's own health bar and get-up bar over each Helldiver can be hidden, since the bars under the cards show the same. Both are hidden at first.

Needs Obsidian API 1.11.0 or later for Mod Settings. With an older one, every part stays on.
