# Better HUD

A mod for HELLDIVERS (2015) that adds to the game's HUD, for [Obsidian Mod Manager](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianModManager). Each part can be turned on or off in the pause menu, under **Mod Settings → Better HUD**.

- **Ammo counter:** over the magazine count, the rounds left in the magazine, laser heat (red near overheating) or arc charge.
- **Player health bars:** a red bar under each player card, in missions, with current and maximum health at the left. White chunks fade out where health was lost, as on the game's bars. While a player is downed, the bar turns dark red as they bleed out, with a yellow bar under it showing how close they are to getting back up.
- **Enemy health bars:** a bar over every damaged enemy, or only damaged heavies. Bug bars are orange, cyborg bars red and Illuminate bars purple. White chunks fade out where health was lost, turning orange while the enemy burns and green while it's poisoned, and the bar pulses blue while it's stunned. Ticks mark every 100 health, bigger enemies get bigger bars, and a bar flashes dark red when the enemy dies. Bars follow the UI scale setting.
- **Bars over Helldivers:** the health and recovery bars the game draws over each Helldiver can be hidden, since the bars under the cards show the same. Both are hidden at first.

Needs Obsidian API 1.11.0 or later for Mod Settings. With an older one, every part stays on.
